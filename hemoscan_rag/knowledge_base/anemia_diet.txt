Iron-Rich Foods (Heme Iron - High Absorption)
Organ Meats: Beef liver, chicken liver, pork liver, kidney.

Red Meat: Beef (chuck roast, steak, ground beef), lamb, venison, goat.

Poultry: Chicken (dark meat/thighs), turkey (dark meat), duck.

Seafood: Clams, mussels, oysters, sardines (canned in oil), anchovies, shrimp, mackerel, tuna, halibut, haddock, salmon.

Iron-Rich Foods (Non-Heme Iron - Moderate Absorption)
Leafy Greens: Spinach, kale, Swiss chard, collard greens, dandelion greens, beet greens.

Legumes: Lentils, chickpeas (garbanzo beans), black beans, kidney beans, navy beans, pinto beans, soybeans (edamame), lima beans.

Nuts and Seeds: Pumpkin seeds (pepitas), sesame seeds, hemp seeds, flaxseeds, cashews, pine nuts, pistachios, sunflower seeds, almonds.

Tofu and Soy: Firm tofu, tempeh, natto, fortified soy milk.

Grains: Fortified breakfast cereals, amaranth, quinoa, oats, enriched rice, whole wheat bread, teff.

Dried Fruits: Apricots, raisins, prunes, figs, dates, peaches.

Vegetables: Broccoli, asparagus, potatoes (with skin), mushrooms (white button and morel), green peas, tomato paste.

Sweeteners: Blackstrap molasses.

Vitamin C Boosters (To Enhance Iron Absorption)
Fruits: Oranges, grapefruits, lemons, limes, strawberries, kiwis, mangoes, papayas, pineapple, cantaloupe.

Vegetables: Red and green bell peppers, broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cauliflower, tomatoes, cabbage, snow peas.

Folate (Vitamin B9) Sources
Greenery: Asparagus, spinach, romaine lettuce, mustard greens.

Legumes: Black-eyed peas, lentils, pinto beans.

Fruit: Avocados, oranges, bananas.

Grains: Enriched pasta, fortified bread.

Vitamin B12 Sources
Animal Products: Eggs (specifically the yolk), whole milk, yogurt, cottage cheese, Swiss cheese, cheddar cheese.

Plant-Based (Fortified): Nutritional yeast, fortified plant milks (almond, soy, oat), fortified cereals.

Inhibitors (Foods to Avoid During High-Iron Meals)
Tannins and Polyphenols: Black tea, green tea, coffee, cocoa, red wine.

Calcium-Rich Foods: Milk, cheese, yogurt, calcium supplements (should be consumed 2 hours apart from iron).

Phytates: Unsoaked beans, raw grains, soy protein isolates.

Oxalates: Large amounts of chocolate, parsley, rhubarb.

Sample High-Iron Meal Combinations
Breakfast: Fortified oatmeal topped with sliced strawberries and pumpkin seeds.

Lunch: Spinach salad with grilled chicken, orange segments, and sunflower seeds.

Dinner: Beef or lentil stew cooked with tomatoes, bell peppers, and potatoes (skin on).

Snack: Dried apricots and cashews or hummus with sliced red bell peppers.

Cooking Methods
Cast Iron Cookware: Preparing acidic foods (like tomato sauce) in cast iron skillets to increase elemental iron content.

Sprouting and Soaking: Soaking beans and grains to reduce phytates.

Leavening: Using leavened bread rather than unleavened to improve mineral availability.
